# Francis of Assisi: An Unpossessed Life
## WisdomForge Booklet — Adult

---

## About This Book

Adults do not need another birdbath. They need a man who made money nervous, made war look theologically illiterate, and then told his own brothers not to explain him into something they could fund.

Francis of Assisi (1181/82–1226) left a short corpus. Treat it as a veto. Armstrong and Brady’s *Francis and Clare: The Complete Works* is the English door. Thomas of Celano (1228) is early official memory. Augustine Thompson (2012) is the biography that will not let you rest in a fresco. John Tolan (2009) will show you your own century hiding in Damietta. David Burr and Malcolm Lambert will not let poverty stay a mood.

This band is for rooms you actually inhabit: a parish finance meeting, a family inheritance, a hospital corridor, a climate board, an interfaith dinner, a hiring conversation about “culture fit.” Practice items are protocols with deliverables. They are not pastoral care and not medical advice.

Six chapters, same spine as the younger bands, different job: you get the fights, the documents, and the places where reception becomes recruitment.

## Chapter 1: The Palate, Not the Brand

Francis’s own origin story is not a brand deck. It is a change in taste.

> “The Lord gave me, Brother Francis, thus to begin doing penance in this way: for when I was in sin, it seemed too bitter a thing to see lepers. And the Lord Himself led me among them and I showed mercy to them. And when I departed from them, what had seemed bitter to me was changed into sweetness of soul and body. And afterwards I delayed a little and left the world.”
> — *Testament* (1226)

Adults love origin stories that can be scheduled. This one cannot. The Lord leads. Mercy is shown. The body — not only the opinions — flips from bitter to sweet. Then delay. Then leaving. If your conversion narrative has no delay, you may be narrating a rebrand.

Medieval leprosy was a social death: walls, bells, dissolved rights, a theology of visible curse (Demaitre; the civil/canon practices Thompson assumes). Coming close is not “volunteering.” It is crossing a purity regime. Wolf’s question — did Francis need the leper more than the leper needed Francis? — belongs in an adult room because adults know how to use other people’s pain as spiritual capital. The *Testament* does not do a victory lap. That is evidence. It is not an acquittal. The leper still becomes an origin forever. Hold both.

Collestrada and the Perugia prison (1202–3) emptied knighthood. They did not teach him whom to touch. Assisi’s *minores* had already won a commune fight in 1198. He is not a peasant with no other move. He is a winner’s son leaving the win. Pietro cannot parse that as charity because charity, in a shop, leaves the shop standing.

Celano will offer a speaking crucifix at San Damiano. Francis’s last will does not. Adult reading privileges the will for beginnings. You may still love the crucifix scene. You may not let it overrule the document the dying man chose.

Scholarly debate, at adult length: ethical conversion versus mystical conversion is a false fork here. The text is mystical *because* it is carnal — sweetness of soul *and body*. A seminar that cannot hear “the Lord led me” will sociologize him into a case. A parish that cannot hear Wolf will saint him into a user of the poor. Neither room is honest alone.

Hagiography is five jobs, not one book. Layer 1 is Francis’s own corpus — Canticle, Testament, Rules, letters, prayers — a veto, not a memoir. Layer 2 is Celano’s *Vita Prima* (1228–29), commissioned after canonization, already selecting for a saint, then *Vita Secunda* (1247) to correct and thicken from further testimony. Layer 3 is Bonaventure’s *Legenda Maior* (1263): a Minister General’s unification, an *alter Christus* coherent enough to hold an order already splitting over poverty, and the sidelining of messier earlier lives. Layer 4 is the *Fioretti*, late fourteenth century, more than a hundred years out: wolf, birds, crib as folk devotion. Layer 5 is Thompson and the *Early Documents* project, which treat the layers as evidence rather than as a single fresco. Conversion is the test case. Celano needs a speaking crucifix and a kiss. Francis, dying, names a leper and a delay. Letting Layer 2 overrule Layer 1 is not “richer.” It is a different beginning.

Conservative readers inside the tradition hold that early narrative cores are sound even when miracles are later paint. Critical readers strip hagiographic jobs to recover a reformer who is more human and less miraculous. Hermeneutic readers refuse the strip: how Francis was perceived is part of what he historically became. You need all three methods. None of them gets to invent a saying. The *Testament*’s “the Lord gave me” formula — conversion, churches, brothers, Gospel form, priests, peace greeting — is Matura’s received vocation, not a personal brand. Adults who have rewritten their origin stories for a hiring packet should hear the difference. Received is not passive. He walks, strips, sails, hides wounds. Received is the opposite of sovereign.

Collestrada and Perugia emptied knighthood of glamour. They did not teach him whom to touch. Assisi’s *minores* had already won in 1198. He is a winner’s son leaving the win. Pietro cannot parse that as charity because charity, in a shop, leaves the shop standing. Social death (Demaitre; civil and canon practice) means the leprosaria sit outside walls for a reason: property, marriage, church access, a theology of visible curse. Coming close is not volunteering. It is crossing a purity regime. Wolf’s question — did Francis need the leper more than the leper needed Francis? — belongs at an adult table because adults know how to use other people’s pain as spiritual capital. The *Testament* does not do a victory lap. That is evidence. It is not an acquittal. The leper still becomes an origin forever. Hold both, or you will get a tract.

The founding sentence belongs beside the leper, because the same grammar of gift structures both:

> “And after the Lord gave me some brothers, no one showed me what I should do, but the Most High Himself revealed to me that I should live according to the form of the Holy Gospel. And I had this written down simply and in few words, and the Lord Pope confirmed it for me.”
> — *Testament*

Three agents: Most High, Francis as scribe of few words, pope as confirmer. Remove any one and you get a different movement: pure interiority, founder-cult, or curia. Adults who have founded a nonprofit, a parish ministry, or a company will recognize the triangle and how it stops being equilateral. Delay is still doctrine. The brothers arrive after the leper, not instead of the leper. A room that wants a scalable origin without a socially dead body is already glossing.

Hospital corridors make this expensive. You already have a protocol below. The point of putting it after the triangle is not to turn Francis into a chaplaincy manual. It is to notice that your nervous system still has a bell — the signal that says “approach carefully” — and that Francis named the bell as the beginning of theology. Wolf remains in the room: using the corridor as your turning-point story, without the people in it getting a life after your turning, is spiritual capital. The *Testament* does not acquit you. It only refuses a conversion that never had to come close.

Adults love origin stories that can be scheduled. This one cannot. If your conversion narrative has no delay, you may be narrating a rebrand. A hiring packet that turns a leper into a “formative experience” without remainder is Wolf’s question in a suit. Privilege the will for beginnings even when you love Celano’s crucifix. You may not let a set piece overrule the document the dying man chose. The first Franciscan theology is proximity to the socially dead, received as gift, undergone as a change in the nervous system. Delay is part of the doctrine. So is the body. Dualism loses. So does a purely sociological reduction that cannot hear “the Lord led me.” Neither a seminar nor a parish is honest alone.

### Big Idea

The first Franciscan theology is proximity to the socially dead, received as gift, undergone as a change in the nervous system. Delay is part of the doctrine.

### Practice

Afterward, write 400 words on what your body did — recoil, hurry, joke, phone. Do not write a moral. Deliverable: the 400 words, unread by the people you watched.
2. **Inheritance table.** At the next family money conversation, notice who is treated as already dead to the decision. One page to yourself after. No sending it to the group chat.
3. **Read-aloud.** Read the *Testament* leper paragraph aloud to one other adult. Ask them what verb they do not believe. Write their verb down.
4. **Thompson, one chapter** on the conversion years. List two places he will not follow Celano. Deliverable: two citations.

## Chapter 2: Coin as a Spiritual Technology

Lady Poverty is courtly love aimed at the thing a merchant’s town exists to flee. Adults should hear the class: Pietro’s wealth is coin, credit, cloth — the new measure — not only land.

> “And let the brothers not appropriate anything to themselves, neither house nor place nor anything. And as pilgrims and strangers in this world, serving the Lord in poverty and humility, let them go with confidence to seek alms.”
> — Later Rule, ch. 6

Pilgrim and stranger are not aesthetics. They are a legal mood: you do not file a deed on the inn. “Spiritual friends” who hold the coin you will not touch are the adult problem in seed. You have sat on boards that keep the mission sentence and move the money offstage. You know the elegance. You know the leak. Later Rule 4 writes the nerve and then the valve:

> “I strictly command all my brothers not to receive coins or money in any form, either personally or through intermediaries. Nevertheless, the ministers and custodians alone may take special care through their spiritual friends to provide for the needs of the sick and the clothing of the others according to places, seasons and cold climates, as they judge necessary, saving always that, as stated above, they do not receive coins or money.”
> — Later Rule, ch. 4

The first sentence is absolute. The second keeps it and routes coin through friends and officers. Spirituals will call the route a fiction. Conventuals will call it how a hospital gets blankets. Francis is already inside the contradiction while alive. Chapter 5 then refuses idle holiness:

> “Those brothers to whom the Lord has given the grace of working may work faithfully and devotedly so that, while avoiding idleness, the enemy of the soul, they do not extinguish the Spirit of holy prayer and devotion to which all temporal things must contribute. In payment for their work they may receive whatever is necessary for the bodily support of themselves and their brothers, excepting coin or money, and let them do this humbly as is becoming for servants of God and followers of most holy poverty.”
> — Later Rule, ch. 5

Pay as food and cloth, not as coin. Prayer as the thing work must not kill. That is a different economy from a wage as proof of worth and a different economy from a professional beggar with a holy face.

> “And I worked with my hands, and I still desire to work; and I firmly wish that all my brothers give themselves to honest work. … And when we are not paid for our work, let us have recourse to the Lord’s table, begging alms from door to door.”
> — *Testament*

Idle holiness and hoarding craft are both refused. Adult economies make wage the proof of worth. Francis will not. He also will not bless a professional beggar with a holy face. The pairing scrapes. Let it scrape.

The square in Assisi is a legal act: clothes returned, father refused, bishop’s cloak accepted. Adults who have left a family system will recognize the cost without needing a fresco.

Lambert and Burr: after his death the argument is whether Christ and the apostles owned nothing even corporately, whether *usus pauper* is a life or a slogan, whether a papal trust is fidelity or gloss. The Rule of 1223 says appropriate nothing, go as pilgrims and strangers. The Testament is hotter: work with hands, no special privileges, lesser brothers. “Not appropriating” can mean two things. Individual non-ownership with a corporate body that still holds churches and books. Or no ownership at all — not even the order — only use as borrowed. Francis’s practice leaned toward the second while the movement was small. Growth required roofs. Spirituals insisted on absolute poverty, individual and corporate, and on *usus pauper* as a visible life. Some edges mixed the claim with Joachim of Fiore’s age of the Spirit, which made the papacy itself look like unrepented wealth. Conventuals answered that preaching and study need schools, schools need buildings, and a remnant too pure to preach has lost both mission and poverty. Nicholas III’s *Exiit Qui Seminat* (1279) tried elegance: the order owns nothing; the pope holds property in trust; the brothers have use. Spirituals heard a legal fiction. Conventuals heard too much concession to rigorists. John XXII’s *Cum Inter Nonnullos* (1323) declared it heretical to say that Christ and the apostles owned nothing. Marseille 1318 burned men who would not recant. Ockham fled to Louis of Bavaria and accused the pope. This is not a book club. Francis was not a jurist of corporate property. He left a practice and a veto without a treatise. Both later parties quote him because the documents are genuinely tense. A dozen men can own nothing. Several thousand men need infrastructure. Both sides had a case. If your Francis never becomes expensive to a treasury, you are still in a garden. The modern split — OFM, Conventual, Capuchin — still names the argument. Capuchin reform (1525) is the nearest institutional cousin of the Spiritual nerve, and even Capuchins own corporately.

Lady Poverty is courtly love aimed at the thing a merchant’s town exists to flee. Adults should hear the class twice: Pietro’s wealth is coin, credit, cloth — the new measure — not only land. An inheritance table is the modern square. Who is already dead to the decision. Which object, if returned in public, would annihilate the story you tell about yourself. Pride in patches is already infidelity; poverty that looks in the mirror has married a different lady. Joy (*laetitia*) is the lie-detector for grim products and for cute brands. Grimness as proof is a product. Holy foolishness is un-buyability. A finance committee that keeps the mission sentence while moving possession offstage is Later Rule 4’s “nevertheless” in khakis. You know the elegance. Name the leak before you perform a takedown you will not survive.

Clare is not a helper in the margin. She will be less patient with loopholes. Name her so the men’s order does not eat the evidence. A Francis booklet that never lets a woman be more radical than Francis is doing the order’s old work.

The public nude scene before Bishop Guido is a legal act: clothes returned, paternal authority refused, ecclesiastical covering accepted. Pietro is not a cartoon. He is a merchant watching inventory become masonry for a chapel that will not profit him. Family rupture is the cost, not a side effect. Adults who have left a family system will recognize it without a fresco. Coin is a spiritual technology because it trains a palate: what counts as safety, as adulthood, as love. Francis smashed a specific palate — mercantile coin as salvation — and left a veto institutions cannot stop quoting or obeying. *Usus pauper* as a visible life versus *usus* as a legal mood is the whole later war in seed. If you can already see how a valve becomes a pipeline, you are ready for Burr. If you cannot, you are still treating poverty as a feeling. Work remains the pairing that scrapes a résumé culture: example and the killing of idleness, not a wage as proof of worth, not a holy face on a professional beggar. The Lord’s table after unpaid work is a wager that God still feeds. Test the wager and you will discover how much of your courage is a grocery store.

A board packet is a gloss machine. Mission sentences survive; possession moves offstage; spiritual friends appear as “restricted funds,” “supporting organizations,” “friends of.” Later Rule 4 already wrote your minutes. The first sentence is absolute. The second is how blankets arrive. Both sentences are his. The later war is which sentence you are allowed to treat as the real one. *Exiit* tries to keep both by making the pope the owner. *Cum Inter Nonnullos* decides that a certain poverty thesis is heresy. Marseille is what happens when a legal mood becomes a pyre. You do not get to treat that as colorful medieval background if you sit on a treasury. Name the valve. Name the nerve. Do not call them identical.

### Big Idea

Ownership trains a palate. Francis smashed a specific palate — mercantile coin as salvation — and left a veto (“without gloss”) that institutions cannot stop quoting or obeying.

2. **Closet protocol.** Give away one object whose absence will change how you tell your story. Not a duplicate mug.
3. **Two-chair.** Spiritual friends as wisdom vs. as fiction. Record yourself for five minutes each side. Listen once.
4. **Read Later Rule 6** in Armstrong/Brady. Copy it by hand. Margin: where your household already glosses.

## Chapter 3: The Triangle — Most High, Few Words, Pope

San Damiano’s voice is Celano. Francis’s churches are a prayer:

> “We adore You, Lord Jesus Christ, here and in all Your churches throughout the whole world, and we bless You, because by Your holy cross You have redeemed the world.”
> — *Testament*

And the early petition:

> “Most High, glorious God, enlighten the darkness of my heart and give me true faith, certain hope, and perfect charity, sense and knowledge, Lord, so that I may carry out Your holy and true command.”
> — Prayer before the Crucifix

Adults who have left churches because buildings disappointed them should sit with “all Your churches.” Repair is intramural. The Eucharist sentence, from the *Testament*, makes the dropout myth expensive:

> “I see nothing corporally in this world of the Son of God except His most holy Body and Blood, which they receive and which they alone administer to others.”
> — *Testament*

The *Letter to the Entire Order* is a second witness, not a restatement. Dying, he kisses their feet on paper and aims them at the altar:

> “Wherefore, brothers, kissing your feet and with the charity of which I am capable, I conjure you all to show all reverence and all honor possible to the most holy Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ, in whom the things that are in heaven and the things that are on earth are pacified and reconciled to Almighty God.”
> — *Letter to the Entire Order*

He is a deacon. He does not found a parallel altar. He also does not let clergy become décor. The proto-Protestant reading fails here in public, not only in a Grown-Up appendix. Francis submitted to papal approval, revered the consecrated host as real presence, accepted confession and priests, placed Portiuncula under Mary, and never challenged Catholic doctrine. He challenged clerical wealth, rigidity, lay exclusion, and distance from the poor. Those are reforms of practice from inside a Church he would not leave. Luther is not a delayed Francis. Francis is not an early Luther. Claiming him as a walkout is reception. Layer 1 will not fund it.

The form of life:

> “The Rule and life of the Friars Minor is this: to observe the Holy Gospel of Our Lord Jesus Christ, living in obedience, without anything of one’s own, and in chastity.”
> — Later Rule, ch. 1

1209 oral approval. 29 November 1223, *Solet Annuere*. Between them: thousands, novitiate, examined preachers, study with a knife in it:

> “I am pleased that you read sacred theology to the brothers, provided that you do not extinguish the spirit of prayer and devotion during this study.”
> — Letter to Anthony of Padua

Later Rule 9 turns charismatic speech into an office:

> “The brothers may not preach in the diocese of any bishop when he has opposed their doing so. And let none of the brothers dare to preach in any way to the people unless he has been examined and approved by the minister general of this fraternity and the office of preacher has been conferred upon him. I also admonish and exhort those brothers that when they preach their language be well-considered and chaste for the benefit and edification of the people, announcing to them vices and virtues, punishment and glory, with brevity, because our Lord when on earth kept his word brief.”
> — Later Rule, ch. 9

Short virtuous words. A bishop’s no is a no. That is not automatically betrayal. It is what a real order required. Francis’s heat in 1226 is the fear that office will eat the form. Heat is evidence. It is not a secret fourth Rule.

Resignation as minister general (~1220) plus a dying veto:

> “I strictly command all my brothers, cleric and lay, by obedience, not to put any gloss or interpretation on the Rule or on these words.”

He wants confirmation *and* no commentary. Adult institutions want one of those. You have written policies and then written FAQs that undo them. That FAQ is a gloss. He also calls the Testament a remembrance, not another Rule:

> “And let the brothers not say: ‘This is another Rule,’ because this is a remembrance, an admonition, an exhortation, and my testament, which I, Brother Francis, your little one, make for you, my blessed brothers.”
> — *Testament*

He wants the bull *and* the veto on commentary. History rarely honors both. The hermitage rule (Martha/Mary rotation) is the structural critique of specialist holiness. Contemplation and dishes trade places. A movement that only preaches becomes a circus. A movement that only hides becomes a club. The same three people stacking chairs forever is already a class system. If you lead, the threat is not laziness. It is being indispensable. Indispensability is a possession.

Dalarun and Thompson on San Damiano: how much is later theology of a speaking Christ? Adult answer: love the scene, privilege the silence in the will, do not call that hatred of churches. Portiuncula and San Damiano are leftover sacred space. Reform starts where budgets stopped. Clare’s later dwelling at San Damiano means the “house” becomes a women’s house — a fact men’s official lives under-narrate. If repair is only a male order with papal privileges, the house is still falling.

Study’s fuse-sentence to Anthony must be read as permission *and* limit. Bonaventure, Scotus, Ockham are not betrayals of a man who hated thought. They are what happens when prayer is told not to die during theology. The death of prayer in a classroom — or in a board packet — is still the named danger. Obedience to the Roman Church in the Later Rule is why he is not available as a mascot of pure dissent. It is also why later popes could bind the order against the Spirituals. You cannot cheer Innocent III’s yes in 1209 and treat John XXII as a random villain without watching the structure you cheered.

Policy without FAQ is the adult form of “without gloss.” Delete the explanatory paragraph that makes a rule cheap, and sit with the cost. A disappointing building is the adult San Damiano: sixty seconds of the adoration prayer in a church you do not like, private notes on resistance, not a social post. Rotation of Mary and Martha is the adult hermitage: if you lead, swap once and write what threatened you. The Anthony knife is the adult classroom: a timer where work stops for undistracted silence, and an honest note on whether you used study to flee. These are not pastoral assignments. They are source protocols translated into rooms you already inhabit. Gloss is the named enemy, not institution. He sought a papal yoke and forbade the interpretation that makes yokes wearable.

Read the Testament twice: once for the leper, once for the triangle of Most High / few words / pope. If your notes from the two readings do not fight each other, you read too fast. The adoration prayer in “all Your churches” is already a theology of presence: Christ is not privately owned by the enthusiast. A spirituality that cannot enter a disappointing building is not his. The Eucharist sentence ends the dropout myth and makes him dangerous to a Church that uses sacrament as décor. If the Body is the only corporal presence, then war-camps, markets, and palaces are measured against an altar. That is not quietism. Gloss hunt a code you live under. Write the first clever interpretation people use. Write the “simply and purely” counter. Note the cost. The FAQ you would have written is the temptation.

### Big Idea

Francis sought a papal yoke and forbade the interpretation that makes yokes wearable. Gloss, not institution, is the named enemy.

## Chapter 4: Kinship After the Managerial Age

The Canticle’s grammar will not hire you as a steward-CEO of a dead warehouse.

> “Praised be You, my Lord, with all Your creatures, especially Sir Brother Sun, who is the day and through whom You give us light. And he is beautiful and radiant with great splendor; and bears a likeness of You, Most High One.”

*Through. Likeness.* Not identity. The rest of the Canticle must be on the page, or the birdbath wins by excerpt:

> “Praised be You, my Lord, through Sister Moon and the stars; in heaven You formed them clear and precious and beautiful.
> Praised be You, my Lord, through Brother Wind, and through the air, cloudy and serene, and every kind of weather through which You give sustenance to Your creatures.
> Praised be You, my Lord, through Sister Water, which is very useful and humble and precious and chaste.
> Praised be You, my Lord, through Brother Fire, through whom You light the night; and he is beautiful and playful and robust and strong.
> Praised be You, my Lord, through our Sister Mother Earth, who sustains and governs us, and who produces various fruits with colored flowers and herbs.”
> — *Canticle of the Creatures*

Sister Water is humble and chaste. Sister Mother Earth *governs*. Adults who sit on climate boards should feel the insult to managerial exceptionalism. Stewardship can still loot politely. Kinship makes looting a family crime. Weather includes cloudy days. Fire is playful and dangerous. The pantheist reading fails in public here, not only in a high-band appendix. If Brother Sun *were* God, Francis would not need likeness. Sacrament is not identity. The sun is vestige; Christ is image. Collapse those and you get idolatry dressed as ecology. Keep them without kinship and you get a warehouse. Nature is created, not divine. Praise runs *through* creatures, not *as* them. Sorrell tests whether “nature mystic” survives the preposition. Delio maps sacrament. White (1967) needed an exception to a Christian villain story. He did not need an honorary druid. *Laudato Si’* names Francis “much loved by non-Christians” and still refuses to detach the ethic from “the convictions of our faith.” A board may borrow kinship language. Borrowing is not the Canticle. Say when you have edited the Most High out.

> “Praised be You, my Lord, through those who give pardon for Your love, and bear infirmity and tribulation. Blessed are those who endure in peace, for by You, Most High, shall they be crowned.”

The poem includes civic pardon and, later, Sister Death. Cut those and you have a nature hike. Composition: illness, feud, deathbed. A life in three weathers.

The Canticle’s opening is not optional atmosphere. It names who is praised before any sibling appears:

> “Most High, all-powerful, good Lord, Yours are the praises, the glory, the honor, and all blessing. To You alone, Most High, do they belong, and no man is worthy to mention Your name. Praised be You, my Lord, with all Your creatures…”
> — *Canticle of the Creatures*, opening

A climate board that keeps the siblings and drops the Most High has edited the grammar. *Laudato Si’* (2015) is reception that keeps the Lord in the first line and names integral ecology: one crisis, poor and land, because one habit treats creatures as objects (§139; §§10–12). Greccio: Incarnation not embarrassed by hay. Altar: Body, not likeness. Collapse those levels and you get magic or mush. Brother Ass: load the body, do not hate it, do not display it. He ate meat the Rule allowed. He was not a carbon analyst. Orientation is the gift: gratitude rather than managerial panic, kinship rather than loot. Secularizing kinship: the verses say no. A public square may still borrow. Borrowing is not identity. Say when you have edited God out. The garden statue drops the grammar and keeps the bird. Dante’s *Paradiso* XI is reception at genius level: Poverty as bride, the seal as close. Use Dante as theology of meaning, not as a witness to 1224 flesh.

A household utilities memo that never says “resource” and then drops, in the policy sentence you would actually pass, back into inventory language, is the whole problem in miniature. Kinship makes looting a family crime. Stewardship can still loot politely. Integral ecology’s claim — one crisis, social and environmental — maps onto Francis’s single habit of refusing to treat a creature as object, whether leper or water. That mapping is historically defensible *and* incomplete. Do not hire him as a carbon analyst. Do not fire him as a sentimentalist. The preposition *through* and the noun *likeness* are the brakes. Without them you have either idolatry or a warehouse. With them you have a liturgy that includes cloudy weather, humble water, playful fire, earth that governs, pardon that costs, and death that is a sister. That is not a hike. It is a life.

Read the Canticle aloud, standing, once, outdoors if you can, no music bed. Circle every *You*, underline every creature, box every adjective. Which adjective most threatens how your household uses that creature? Kinship versus stewardship: write a defense of each, then a Franciscan critique of each defense. *Laudato Si’* §§10–12 and 139: quote one sentence he could have sung and one he could not have imagined. Caption a humble vestige — drain, frost, insect — without calling it God and without calling it stuff. These are climate-board practices, not nature mysticism. The Most High stays in the first line or you have edited the poem.

### Big Idea

Franciscan ecology is sacramental and Christological. Without that grammar it is another religion in his coat — sometimes a useful one, never his.

## Chapter 5: A Walk That Converted No One

Peace is not the unattributed poster. It is a revealed greeting.

> “And the Lord revealed to me that I should say the greeting, ‘The Lord give you peace,’ which I previously did not use.”
> — *Testament*

Rank-flattening. John 20 in the street. Cheap if you let it be.

> “And if he should fall a thousand times, let him be received back.”
> — Letter to a Minister

Adults who manage people know exile is efficient. Francis forbids efficiency as the first tool. He does not forbid truth. Reception is not amnesia.

Early Rule 16:

> “And the brothers who go among the Saracens… can live among them spiritually… or, when they see it pleases God, announce the word of God.”

Damietta 1219: Fifth Crusade, Pelagius, hunger, plunder-as-piety (Powell). Francis and Illuminatus cross. Beaten. Al-Kamil — cultured, Saladin’s nephew — listens, in Celano’s “great attention.” Gifts refused. No baptism. Safe return. Warning against attack, ignored. Later fire and secret conversion fail early sources (Tolan).

Letters to rulers: power is temporary; honor the Body; remember the poor. A camp that gambles under a cross is a liturgical crisis before it is a military one. The letter itself, not only its reputation:

> “To all podestàs, and consuls, judges and governors, in whatever part of the world, and to all others to whom this letter may come, Brother Francis, your little and contemptible servant, wishes health and peace to you. Consider and see that the day of death draws nigh. I ask you, therefore, with such reverence as I can, not to forget the Lord on account of the cares and solicitudes of this world and not to turn aside from His commandments… And when the day of death comes, all that which they think they have shall be taken away from them. And the wiser and more powerful they may have been in this world, so much the greater torments shall they endure in hell. Wherefore, I strongly advise you, my lords, to put aside all care and solicitude and to receive readily the most holy Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ in holy commemoration of Him.”
> — *Letter to the Rulers of the Peoples* (c. 1220; Robinson 1905 from Wadding; Armstrong/Brady print the same letter)

Death, stripped possession, Eucharist, evening praise. A barefoot friar writing to people who mint the world. The manuscript tradition is thin. Thin is not fake. Quote carefully; do not invent a longer harangue.

Attractive, speculative. Early Rule 16 permits presence *or* preaching. He picks preaching. Christocentrism is not canceled by courtesy. Courtesy is not pluralism. Keep both. Either cancellation is anachronism with a halo.

## Chapter 6: Gloves, Sister Death, and the Treasury

La Verna, September 1224. Celano’s seraph and marks; companions see; gloves; pain; first widely attested stigmatic. Dante’s “final seal” (*Paradiso* XI) is meaning, not a lab note. He will not write the spectacle. He writes the altar. Hiddenness fits a divine name:

> “You are holy, Lord God, the only God, who does wonderful things. You are strong. You are great. You are the Most High. You are love. You are wisdom. You are humility. You are patience.”
> — *Praises of God* (for Brother Leo)

Display would be a different god. The only surviving autograph is not a wound-report:

> “Blessing and all good, the Lord give you. Brother Francis.”
> — Blessing of Brother Leo (autograph, c. 1224–1225)

Master refused in ink. Esser on the autograph is the scholarly reminder: the one scrap in his hand chooses “Brother.”

Mechanism stays open. The marks were real in the only sense a historian can use: early sources, companions, persistence. Thomas of Celano describes black marks and a side that often bled — more restrained than later art. Interpretation is theological: imitation becoming participation; seraph as burning love united to crucifixion. Medical options remain: psychosomatic Passion-attention (real bodily effects of intense religious experience, poorly studied), pre-existing disease (malaria, malnutrition, possible tuberculoid lesions consistent with known illness), miracle. Self-infliction is possible in theory and poorly matched to the sources. We do not have enough evidence to diagnose from eight centuries. Meaning does not wait on mechanism. Glibness — credulous or sneering — is the only forbidden affect. The stigmata introduced a new criterion of sanctity: bodily conformity during life, not only wonders at a tomb (Vauchez). After Francis, cases multiply; his is the archetype. Doubt is as old as the claim. This booklet will not close a courtroom. It will not pretend the question is unserious.

> “Praised be You, my Lord, through our Sister Bodily Death, from whom no living man can escape. Woe to those who die in mortal sin. Blessed are those whom death will find in Your most holy will, for the second death shall do them no harm.”
> — Canticle, last movement

Woe and blessing. Not slush. Adults near deathbeds: Sister Death is not a joke and not a denial. It is unclutching as praise. It is also a warning. Do not use it as clinical guidance.

Then the war over a will. Without gloss versus roofs. Return to the poverty controversy at adult length, because the wounds did not settle it. Spirituals claimed Christ and the apostles owned nothing, neither individually nor corporately, so every friary and library owned by the order was a Gospel betrayal. Conventuals claimed the Apostolic life includes preaching, and preaching on a continent requires books and roofs, held if need be by the pope in trust. *Exiit Qui Seminat* (1279) satisfied neither camp. *Cum Inter Nonnullos* (1323) ended the legal fiction by making a poverty thesis heresy. Marseille 1318. Ockham in exile accusing a pope. The Testament was judged not a legal document. Charismatic authority lost to institutional authority inside the house he founded. Both sides had a case. Francis was not a jurist of corporate property. Growth made the contradiction; the veto made it explosive. OFM / Conventual / Capuchin still name the split.

Hagiography layers again at the end: Celano writes a canonization; Bonaventure unifies and sidelines mess; *Fioretti* makes a folk hero; Thompson weighs. Closer to Layer 1: anxious, sacramental, commanding, un-cute. Conversion, San Damiano, Damietta, and La Verna all change shape as you climb. If you only read Layer 4, you will love him and not know him. If you only read Layer 5, you will know about him and never hear him.

Open questions, named rather than listed. **Clare and gender.** Clare (1194–1253) heard him, renounced inheritance, founded San Damiano. The partnership is real. The limits are real. He did not write her a Rule that stuck; Hugolino’s Rule replaced his Form of Life. She fought her life for the Privilege of Poverty and received it on her deathbed. He supported her spiritually and did not build her the male order’s legal house. Mixed-gender mendicancy would have been a scandal that could destroy the movement. The practical decision had theological consequences: the institutional Franciscan vision became a male vision. Clare’s own writings show a partner who pushed poverty further than the brothers’ Rule. Whether the tradition has treated her as co-founder rather than helper remains open. **Reluctant authority.** He founded, wrote rules, served as minister general, resigned ~1220. The Testament reads anxious. By institutional standards that looks like failure. By charismatic founding it may be inevitable. Resignation may be theology (a governor of thousands cannot be a lesser brother) or overwhelm. The sources do not cleanly distinguish. **Crusade compromise.** He walked with papal-army sanction, did not preach against the Crusade, crossed to talk, did not denounce holy war as such. Peace is personal and relational, not a later peace-church theory of non-violence. **Christocentric limits.** He went to the sultan as a Christian who believed Christ is the truth, not as a pluralist. Respectful disagreement is not relativism. Kinship includes Muslims in principle; Christocentrism means the kinship is finally through Christ. Practice at court suggests honor; theology suggests subsumption. The tension is unresolved.

These are not contradictions to dissolve. They prevent easy appropriation. He is not an environmentalist, feminist, pacifist, pluralist, or Protestant in the modern senses. He is a thirteenth-century Catholic mystic and reformer whose legacy is rich enough to speak and not so malleable that it can be anything. The Francis who can be claimed by everyone is worth nothing.

Reception is not reportage. Bonaventure systematizes vestige in the *Itinerarium*; that is a theology of the Canticle, not a replacement for it. Dante stages Poverty as bride. *Laudato Si’* keeps the opening and names integral ecology. The *Fioretti* must be labeled. Layer 1 vetoes the statue. Adults near a will, an advance directive, a treasury, a library question — should a preaching mission own a building? — are in the same contradiction the order never resolved. Steelman yes and no. End with the sentence you still cannot say. Unclutching includes reputation. Gloves hid wounds. A joy that needs an audience is already a coin. Sister Death’s woe clause is not optional. This is not hospice protocol. It is praise that refuses both denial and despair.

Solidarity with the poor is proximity, not charity-as-distance. Lady Poverty without lepers is an aesthetic. Lepers without Lady Poverty can become a volunteer hour that leaves the shop standing. He insists on both, which is why modern readers split him into a social worker and a mystic. Split him and you can hire half of him. He did not offer halves. Incarnation is not a nature hobby bolted onto a separate Jesus hobby. The manger and the cross are the same claim: God did not save the world by remaining an idea. Greccio’s animals are a polemic against a spirituality too refined for straw. The stigmata, whatever their mechanism, are the same polemic at the other end of the life: not too refined for wounds. Brother Ass is a working animal you load and do not romanticize. Asceticism that hates the body is not his. Asceticism that spoils the body to impress heaven is the pride he feared. The *Testament*’s sweetness of *body* after the leper is the positive form of the same anthropology.

You will want him on your side of a present fight: climate, war, inequality, church reform, interfaith, animal ethics, anti-intellectual populism, traditional liturgy. He will almost fit. “Almost” is the trap. If your cause needs him to have written the Peace Prayer, you are recruiting. If it needs the sultan baptized, you are recruiting. If it needs him to hate priests, you are recruiting. If it needs him harmless, you are recruiting. Recruitment is not reception. Learn the difference or every citation is a costume.

Source ladder on paper: Testament, Celano, Bonaventure, *Fioretti*, *Laudato Si’*. Mark veto versus reception. The blessing four lines long that does not steal Leo’s autograph and does not pretend sanctity is harder than it sounds. A library question asked to a colleague who disagrees, steeled on both sides, ending in the sentence you still cannot say, is the poverty controversy without costumes. Sister Death read aloud once, not in public, will show you which clause you wanted to skip — woe or blessing. Skipping is data. Mechanism stays open. Clare stays named. The Testament stays overruled and still binding as remembrance. That pair is the adult Francis.

Celano (*Vita Prima*) and Bonaventure (*Legenda Maior* 13) are the stigmata’s narrative engines. Brother Leo’s independent line matters because official lives have jobs. Francis would not write the spectacle. You should notice. Afterlife of the will: *Exiit* tries to save radical poverty with a legal fiction elegant enough to satisfy neither camp. *Cum Inter Nonnullos* ends the fiction by sacramentalizing property into Christ’s own life — a theological nuclear option. Ockham’s flight shows a Franciscan intellectual tradition willing to accuse a pope. That is the Anthony permission under maximum stress. Vauchez on afterlife of a medieval saint is the scholarly version of Dante’s warning: meaning is not a witness to flesh. Thompson will not let you be glib. Neither should this booklet. Glib is the only forbidden scholarly affect.

Adults near deathbeds and treasuries occupy the same grammar: unclutching. One is breath. One is deed. Francis’s end matches his beginning. Institutions fight because the gift was real enough to fund and therefore to betray. The centuries are not a later accident. They are what happens when a veto is believed.

Four misconceptions, argued on the page rather than parked. **Nature worshipper.** The Canticle’s *through* and *likeness* are the proof. If Francis were a pantheist, Brother Sun would not bear a likeness; he would be the thing itself. Sacramental cosmology is not paganism with better PR.
